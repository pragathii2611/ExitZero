Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, specifically the Job Descriptions you input for analysis. We do not store credit card information; payments are processed securely by our third-party provider (LemonSqueezy).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the text you provide solely to generate the project strategy and code artifacts using Artificial Intelligence (Google Gemini API). We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Local Storage

Your project history is stored locally on your device (in your browser's LocalStorage). We do not maintain a central database of your projects unless explicitly stated.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Gemini for AI generation and Render.com for hosting. Please review their privacy policies for more information on how they handle data processing.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at support@exitzero.in.
